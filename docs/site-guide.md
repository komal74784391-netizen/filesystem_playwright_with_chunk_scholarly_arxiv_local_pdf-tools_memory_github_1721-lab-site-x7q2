# Site guide

- `README.md` is the public landing page. It must stay self-contained: a
  reader should not need to open another page for the essentials.
- `data/publications.json` is the canonical export of the group bibliography.
- Entries in the Publications section are human-curated; they must remain
  consistent with the registry export.
- The PI is highlighted with `<mark>...</mark>`; names sharing equal
  contribution carry `‡` immediately after the name.
- `docs/team.md` holds the roster and general lab information.
